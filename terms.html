Using MicProbe

MicProbe is provided "as is" for testing and diagnostic purposes. Use at your own discretion.

Free microphone tests have daily limits, renewed at midnight UTC. Signing in provides more free tests; a guest test already completed that day counts toward your free account's daily allowance. Failed or insufficient recordings do not count. Lifetime Premium is a single payment with unlimited microphone tests and no recurring membership charge. Reopening a result, listening and downloading an existing recording do not use a test.

Anonymous testing is also limited to one successful test per IP address per UTC day. People sharing an internet connection can sign in to use their separate account allowances. Changing your IP address is not proof of a new person.

New saved reports are a Premium feature. Premium accounts can save up to 200 reports within 10 MiB, whichever limit is reached first. Storage includes measurements, the saved evaluation, notes and any existing earlier reviews. Reaching a limit does not delete existing reports or prevent microphone testing. Remove older saved reports to make room; unsaved results remain on the current browser subject to its storage availability. A change in Premium status does not automatically delete saved reports, and earlier Free-account reports remain available to read and delete.

Your account and saved reports

Sign in with the same MicProbe account to retrieve saved reports and linked premium access. Saved reports contain measurements and settings; they do not include audio recordings. Each report evaluates its own captured test. Previous reports and personal notes do not affect a new result.

Premium details explain measured findings and relevant checks; they do not guarantee a physical diagnosis or a solution. Detailed PDF download requires Premium and is an optional copy of the current report. Reopening a saved evaluation or downloading its PDF does not start a new test. Earlier personal review records are retained as read-only data and do not feed new reports.

What the tests measure

MicProbe's platform profiles are local approximations. A test does not run the actual Discord, Zoom, Teams or other calling-platform client and cannot guarantee how another application will sound. Report suggestions depend on the available measurements and test conditions.
