How it works

Hear the result. Make one change. Check it again.

  1. 1

    Test your microphone

    Choose a local scenario, record a short sample, then listen back and read your free result.

  2. 2

    Make one adjustment

    Use what you heard and your brief result to choose one change, such as speaking distance or microphone settings.

  3. 3

    Test the change

    Record again with the same scenario. Listen for a difference before your next call or voice message.
