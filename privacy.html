Privacy Policy

This page will be updated soon with our complete privacy policy.

Microphone recordings are processed in your browser. Audio recordings are not uploaded to account history. Reports contain measurements, microphone and browser information, captured settings, and any notes you add.

Without signing in, you can test your microphone and view the current result, but reports are not saved to history. When you sign in with Google, MicProbe stores your Google account identifier, name and email to recognize your account. An essential session cookie keeps you signed in.

Reports from tests started while signed in are saved to that account. You can delete individual saved reports from History. Pending saves may remain on this browser until they can be synchronized with their owning account. Signing out hides account history here.

Detailed analysis sends report measurements to the MicProbe server. Freemius processes purchases; MicProbe stores the license association and access status so you can return with the same account. Google and Freemius provide their own privacy information for their services.

For questions, contact us at micprobe@gmail.com.
