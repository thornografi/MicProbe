Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026. This policy describes how MicProbe handles information when you test your microphone, sign in, save reports or purchase Premium.

Who is responsible for your information

MicProbe is operated from Türkiye by Burak ÖZER, the data controller for the information handled through MicProbe. For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact support@micprobe.com.

How and why information is processed

Information is collected electronically through your browser when you use a test, choose settings, sign in or save a report, through Google's sign-in response, and through purchase-verification responses from Freemius. If you contact us, we also receive the information you include in your message.

Account recognition, requested report storage, purchase verification and related support are processed to provide the service you request, on the basis of establishing or performing that service contract under Article 5(2)(c) of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Necessary security and fault-diagnosis processing relies on the legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working, subject to your fundamental rights and freedoms, under Article 5(2)(f). The optional 30-day sign-in cookie is used only when you select it; signing out removes it.

Microphone tests and reports

Microphone recordings are processed in your browser. Audio recordings, PCM samples and transcripts are not uploaded to MicProbe. Reports contain measurements, microphone and browser information, captured settings, scope information, the saved evaluation and any personal notes you add.

Without signing in, you can test your microphone and view the current result, but reports are not saved to your account.

With Premium, you can explicitly save the current guest result to your account. Only that selected report is saved after its connection to this visit is verified. New reports are assessed independently, without earlier reports or personal notes as inputs. Earlier personal review answers and versions remain read-only; they do not affect new reports. Deleting a saved report also deletes its earlier review and removes stored comparison measurements.

Free testing has a daily limit. A first-party micprobe_visitor cookie contains a random browser identifier for up to 30 days; the server stores its hash. Signed-in limits use your account identifier. To manage interrupted tests, this tab may temporarily keep a test identifier and a small measurement summary. Audio recordings are never sent with these requests.

Test-access records contain identifiers, dates and completion status. Old records and expired browser identifiers are removed during subsequent test-access checks. A daily salted hash of the connection IP limits anonymous testing from the same IP address and excessive creation of browser identifiers. Clearing the visitor cookie does not reset this anonymous allowance. People sharing an IP address can sign in to use their separate account allowances. Raw IP addresses are not stored in these records.

Google sign-in and sessions

The home page and test screen check your existing MicProbe session to show the right account and purchase actions. The home page does not load Google's sign-in interface or your saved reports.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your account identifier, name, verified email address and, when available, a profile picture URL. MicProbe stores these to recognize and display your account, associate saved reports with you and link verified purchases. Your picture loads from Google's image service without sending the MicProbe page address as a referrer. If it is unavailable, we display your initials. MicProbe does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or your Google password.

The Google sign-in form selects “Keep me signed in on this device” by default. Leaving it selected saves the sign-in cookie for up to 30 days; unchecking it uses a browser-session cookie. The optional Google One Tap prompt uses a browser-session cookie. Browser session-restore features may also retain session cookies, so sign out on shared devices. Signing out ends that session; it does not delete your saved reports or purchase association. Sign-in security cookies expire after 10 minutes or are removed when sign-in succeeds.

These first-party cookies are named micprobe_session (your signed-in session) and micprobe_login_nonce_* (proof that a sign-in request started in this browser). When accounts are enabled, Google's sign-in service may load as you enter the test screen to offer an optional Google One Tap sign-in, or when you open the sign-in panel. One Tap requires your choice to continue and uses a browser-session cookie; it does not select the optional 30-day sign-in cookie. A flag in this tab's session storage prevents repeated automatic prompts during the same visit. You can dismiss the suggestion and keep testing without an account. Browser settings may prevent the suggestion from appearing.

Saved information and your controls

New reports captured for a Premium account are saved to that account. Free-account results are not added to a new browser or account archive. You can delete individual saved reports from Saved reports. Pending saves remain with their original account; older pending items are not automatically uploaded when you sign in or upgrade. Signing out hides your saved reports on this browser.

Saved reports remain in your account until deleted; MicProbe does not currently apply an automatic report-expiration period. Saved report storage has report-count and storage limits described in the Terms. When full, existing reports are preserved and new Premium results wait on this browser while browser storage is available. If browser storage is also full, the current result remains on the open page and the app reports that it could not be retained. Browser storage also holds preferences, local diagnostic logs and pending account operations. Clearing site data removes those local items and may discard changes that have not synchronized. It does not delete information already saved to your account.

  • Microphone and scenario preferences: the microphone and scenario you choose are remembered on this browser until changed or site data is cleared. No automatic expiry is currently applied.
  • Pending reports and notes: pending report copies stay with their owning account until the save is confirmed or the pending item is removed. A note on an already saved report stays in the open page if its update fails; retry saving before leaving. A small account-bound test identifier is retained after deletion to prevent a delayed save from recreating that report; it contains no measurements or audio.
  • Checkout return: temporary tab storage keeps the current report and scenario while you visit checkout. Restoration is allowed for 30 minutes and consumes the saved copy. A pending purchase return is removed after successful verification. These items otherwise remain until the tab session ends or site data is cleared.
  • Local diagnostic logs: technical events remain on this browser without an automatic expiry. Clearing this site's stored data in your browser settings removes these logs. The app's log-clear command removes messages from the current view; it does not delete the stored logs.
  • Earlier purchase access: if account sign-in is unavailable, verified purchase-access information may be remembered on this browser until replaced or site data is cleared. The server checks access before providing premium details.

Service providers

When Premium-access email notifications are enabled, Resend processes your email address, display name and notification content to send Premium-access confirmations. It also processes support replies written by the MicProbe operator. Audio recordings and saved report measurements are not included in these emails. Replies to these messages go to support@micprobe.com.

MicProbe stores notification identifiers, recipients, a copy for safe retries and delivery status with the account. These records currently have no automatic expiry. Addresses that bounce permanently or report spam are kept on a suppression list to prevent further automated mail until reviewed. Resend also keeps delivery records under its own retention policy.

Detailed analysis sends report measurements to the MicProbe server. The hosted service uses Cloudflare to serve requests and store account data. Hosting providers process network information such as your IP address when serving requests. Google provides sign-in and web fonts. Freemius processes purchases; MicProbe stores the license association and access status so you can return with the same account.

Google Fonts receives your IP address, the font URL requested and browser request headers when fonts load. Google states that its Fonts API does not set or log cookies or use this information for user profiles or targeted advertising. See Google Fonts and privacy.

Checkout and purchase management open on Freemius's site when you choose them. For direct access to purchase management, MicProbe checks your Google identity against the purchase owner and requests a short-lived sign-in link from Freemius. MicProbe does not store that link or a Freemius password. Freemius's payment providers and cookie controls apply there; see Freemius's cookie information. MicProbe does not embed advertising or visitor-analytics scripts in its pages. Service providers may process information outside your country.

See the privacy information from Google, Cloudflare and Freemius for their services.

Contact and account requests

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or a request to delete account information, contact support@micprobe.com. Individual saved reports can be deleted directly from Saved reports.

Depending on the law that applies to you, your rights may include learning whether your information is processed, accessing it, knowing its purposes and recipients, correcting it, requesting deletion or restriction, receiving a portable copy, objecting to processing, and complaining to a data protection authority. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. We may need to verify that a request concerns your own account before acting on it.

Under KVKK Article 11, you may also ask whether information is used in accordance with its stated purpose, request that corrections or deletions be notified to recipients, object to a result against you based solely on automated analysis, and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Requests are handled under the applicable legal conditions and response periods.
